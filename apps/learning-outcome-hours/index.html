How to spread learning outcome hours across the calendar

The planner takes the course start and end dates, the holidays, the class hours for each weekday and the hours for each learning outcome (LO), and works out which days belong to each one. If the calendar doesn’t have enough hours, it shares out the available ones proportionally.

You can paste the data or upload a .txt file, and download the result as CSV to open in a spreadsheet, or as TXT. Spanish and Catalan input formats are also accepted.

Data format

  • Start date: 14/09/2026 and End date: 21/05/2027.
  • Under Holidays and breaks, one date per line or a range such as 22/12/2026 : 07/01/2027.
  • Under Class hours per day, lines such as Tuesday: 2; and under Hours per LO, lines such as LO1: 14.

frequently asked questions

What date format does it use?+

DD/MM/YYYY. Holiday ranges are written as two dates separated by a colon.

What if the learning outcome hours don’t fit in the calendar?+

The planner scales the hours down proportionally and marks them as “Proportional allocation” in the result.

Is my data sent to a server?+

No. Everything is calculated in your browser.
